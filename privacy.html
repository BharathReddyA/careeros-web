This Privacy Policy explains how CareerOS processes your personal data when you use the CareerOS mobile app and the careeros.app website, in accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679, "GDPR").

The short version: we collect what is needed to run your job search agent — your account details, preferences, resumes, applications, and coaching sessions. We use trusted providers to power AI features. We do not sell your data, and we do not use your resumes or coaching conversations to train AI models.

2. What data we collect

2.1 Data you provide

  • Account data: name, email address, password (stored only as a salted hash — we never see or store your plain-text password), optional profile photo.
  • Job preferences: desired job types, locations, and minimum salary.
  • Resume data: resumes you upload, including the personal and professional information they contain (work history, education, skills, contact details).
  • Application data: jobs you save or apply to and the status you record for them.
  • Coaching data: your spoken and written answers during AI interview practice sessions and the feedback generated.

2.2 Data collected automatically

  • Device data: a push notification token for your device (if you enable notifications).
  • Usage data: AI feature usage volume (token counts), used for fair-use limits and billing tiers.
  • Subscription data: a pseudonymous subscriber identifier and subscription status from our subscription provider. We do not receive or store your payment card details — payments are handled entirely by Apple or Google.

2.3 Data we do not collect

We do not collect precise location, contacts, browsing history, or advertising identifiers, and we do not run third-party advertising or tracking in the app.

4. AI processing and automated decision-making

CareerOS uses AI to score job matches, tailor resumes, and conduct voice coaching. Your resume text and coaching responses are sent to our AI providers (see section 5) to generate these outputs. Under our agreements with these providers, your data is used only to provide the service to you and is not used to train their models.

No decisions with legal or similarly significant effects are made about you solely by automated means (GDPR Art. 22). Job match scores are recommendations that order your feed — you always see and choose from available listings yourself, and no employer receives any automated assessment of you from us.

8. Your rights under the GDPR

If you are in the EU/EEA (and in many other jurisdictions), you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access (Art. 15): obtain confirmation of whether we process your data and receive a copy of it.
  • Rectification (Art. 16): have inaccurate data corrected and incomplete data completed. You can edit most data directly in the app.
  • Erasure / "right to be forgotten" (Art. 17): have your data deleted. Deleting your account in the app erases your personal data, subject to statutory retention duties.
  • Restriction (Art. 18): require us to restrict processing in certain circumstances (e.g. while a dispute about accuracy is resolved).
  • Data portability (Art. 20): receive the data you provided to us in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format, and have it transmitted to another controller where technically feasible.
  • Objection (Art. 21): object at any time to processing based on legitimate interests, on grounds relating to your particular situation.
  • Withdraw consent (Art. 7(3)): withdraw any consent (e.g. push notifications) at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.
  • Not to be subject to solely automated decisions (Art. 22): as explained in section 4, we do not make such decisions about you.

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@careeros.app. We will respond within one month (extendable by two further months for complex requests, in which case we will inform you). Exercising your rights is free of charge.

9. Security

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures (Art. 32 GDPR), including:

  • encryption of data in transit (TLS) and at rest;
  • passwords stored only as salted hashes (bcrypt);
  • access controls and authentication tokens for all API access;
  • data processing agreements with all providers;
  • the principle of data minimisation — we collect only what the Service needs.

If a personal data breach occurs that is likely to result in a high risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify you and the competent supervisory authority as required by Articles 33–34 GDPR.

10. Cookies

This website does not set cookies and does not use analytics or tracking technologies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which involves your browser requesting font files from Google's servers; no cookies are set by this request. The mobile app does not use cookies; it uses an authentication token stored securely on your device solely to keep you signed in.

Because we use no non-essential cookies or trackers, no cookie consent banner is required.

11. Children

The Service is not directed at children and requires users to be at least 16 years old. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16. If you believe a child has provided us personal data, contact privacy@careeros.app and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our Service or legal requirements change. For material changes we will notify you via the app or by email before they take effect. The "Last updated" date at the top shows the current version.

13. Complaints — your supervisory authority

If you believe our processing of your data infringes the GDPR, you have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR) — in particular in the EU/EEA member state of your habitual residence, your place of work, or the place of the alleged infringement. A list of national data protection authorities is available at edpb.europa.eu.

We would, however, appreciate the chance to address your concerns first — contact us at privacy@careeros.app.
